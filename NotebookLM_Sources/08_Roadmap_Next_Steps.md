## Decision Log

### Key Architectural Decisions

**Decision 1: Local-First AI**
- **Date**: December 2024
- **Rationale**: Reduce costs, improve privacy, offline capability
- **Trade-offs**: Lower quality than GPT-4, requires more resources
- **Status**: Implemented

**Decision 2: Microservices Architecture**
- **Date**: November 2024
- **Rationale**: Scalability, technology flexibility, team autonomy
- **Trade-offs**: Increased complexity, more infrastructure
- **Status**: Implemented

**Decision 3: Docker-First Development**
- **Date**: October 2024
- **Rationale**: Consistency, easy onboarding, production parity
- **Trade-offs**: Resource usage, learning curve
- **Status**: Implemented
